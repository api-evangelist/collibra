AI regulatory compliance is the work of meeting the legal obligations that apply to how an organization builds, deploys and operates AI. In 2026 that means navigating three moving regimes at once: the EU AI Act, now in its main application phase; a US federal posture centered on preempting state rules; and a patchwork of US state AI laws. The challenge is operating across all three while they shift, and proving you complied.
