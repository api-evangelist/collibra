Agentic AI governance is the practice of controlling autonomous AI agents while they act, not just before they launch. It enforces policy, context and guardrails at runtime, so an agent that books a refund, queries customer data or calls another agent does so within limits you set, with a trace you can defend. Static, point-in-time review can't see a system that acts on its own.
