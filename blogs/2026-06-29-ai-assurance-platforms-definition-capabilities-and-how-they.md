An AI assurance platform is a system that gives continuous confidence that an organization's AI is operating within acceptable risk: detecting risk and drift early, validating behavior in production, and keeping evidence that every model and agent is doing what it should. Where an audit looks backward at whether AI was safe, assurance looks at whether it is safe right now and will stay that way. It's risk management for AI, made continuous.
