Today, building a Data Product means opening three asset pages and filling each by hand. The Data Product, its Ports and the Data Contract get created separately, in whatever order the producer remembers, with no shared draft connecting them. Every attribute, from description to business case to ownership to SLAs, gets typed from scratch even when the underlying table already answers most of it.
