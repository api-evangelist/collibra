When it comes to building, deploying and running technology, data has always had gravity.We can observe this in the way workloads get pulled toward whatever platform holds the storage. Wherever the definitions, the lineage, and the policies accumulate, agents get pulled toward reasoning from that place. In other words, context drives the same pull as data, one layer up.
