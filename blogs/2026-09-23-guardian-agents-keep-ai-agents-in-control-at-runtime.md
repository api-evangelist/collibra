AI agents are moving from experimentation into production — and the stakes are rising fast. An agent can access sensitive data, call tools, interact with enterprise systems, and take actions autonomously. As agent fleets grow, organizations can no longer rely on policies written at deployment or periodic reviews to stay in control.
