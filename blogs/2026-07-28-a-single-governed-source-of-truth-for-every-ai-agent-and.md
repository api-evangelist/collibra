As organizations scale AI agents, the context those systems rely on determine whether they succeed or fail. A developer asking Snowflake Cortex about "monthly active users" may get a different answer than a colleague asking Databricks Genie the same question, because no single governed definition exists to ground either response. According to , 60% of AI projects will be abandoned due to poor data readiness, and missing and fragmented context sits at the heart of that failure.
