AI model monitoring is the continuous tracking of an AI system's performance, inputs and behavior in production, so teams catch degradation, drift and failure before they reach users. It measures whether a model still performs as it did at deployment, whether the data feeding it has shifted, and, for agents, whether the actions it takes stay within expected bounds.
