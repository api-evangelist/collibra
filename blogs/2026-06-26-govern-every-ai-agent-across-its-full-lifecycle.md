AI no longer just generates answers — it now acts. Agents read data, make decisions, call tools and trigger processes across systems, often without a human in the loop. That shift moves risk from “what a model said” to “what an agent did.” Gartner predicts that by 2028, 33% of enterprise software applications will include agentic AI, up from less than 1% in 2024, and that 15% of day-to-day work decisions will be made autonomously.
