AI adoption is outpacing the controls meant to govern it. Every new AI use case, agent, and model increases the complexity of the AI landscape, making it harder for leaders to understand what is running, who owns it, and whether it can be trusted. predicts that by 2027, 60% of organizations will fail to realize the expected value of their AI initiatives due to fragmented governance frameworks.
