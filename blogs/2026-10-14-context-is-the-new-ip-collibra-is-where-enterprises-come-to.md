There’s a context crisis nobody warned you about. While building a context layer for an AI agent could look like a deceptively simple undertaking, what might sound like a low-effort weekend project can quickly bottleneck around sync pipelines, schema mapping, permission layers and staleness guarantees. What AI agents actually need is a context engine that delivers the right context, in the right format, at the right time.
