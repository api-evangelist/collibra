AI audit readiness is the state of having the evidence an auditor will ask for already in place before the audit begins: a complete inventory of your AI, records of how each system was assessed and approved, lineage and audit trails for decisions and actions, and proof that policies were enforced. “Ready” means you produce it on request. “Not ready” means you reconstruct it under deadline, and reconstruction is where findings come from.
